SMA 50 • 50-Day Simple Moving Average

The Institutional Highway & Primary Support Floor
Trend & Moving Averages

📐 Pure Math Formula (KaTeX):
$$ \text{SMA}_{50}(t) = \frac{1}{50} \sum_{i=0}^{49} P_{\text{close}}(t-i) \quad \land \quad P_t > \text{SMA}_{50}(t) > \text{SMA}_{150}(t) $$
💰 Realistic Indian Market Example

If ANANDRATHI has a 50-day average of ₹2,250, a minor dip to ₹2,300 that rebounds with strong volume confirms institutional buyers are eagerly accumulating every pullback.

⚙️ System Invariant (main.py): Rule 02 (Trend Template): Stock price MUST trade strictly above a rising 50-day SMA, and 50 SMA must exceed 150 SMA.
